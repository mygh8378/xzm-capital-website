About XZM Capital LLC

XZM Capital LLC, operating under the DBA Home Expert, is an independent manufacturer's representative focused on North American commercial channel expansion.

We specialize in bridging manufacturers—both domestic and international—with qualified U.S. distributors, dealers, and procurement channels. Our work centers on building practical, long-term commercial relationships rather than one-off transactions.

Based in the Montville / Towaco area of New Jersey, we maintain a practical understanding of the U.S. distribution landscape across several product verticals, including building decorative materials, outdoor products, protective gear, and optical lenses.
